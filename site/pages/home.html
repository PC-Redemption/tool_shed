Token tradeoff: Tool Shed will usually consume more AI tokens than a one-off prompt because it carries more context through planning, implementation, validation, documentation, and follow-through. The tradeoff is intentional: the AI does more of the work and leaves a more complete, reviewable result.

The partnership

You steer. AI works the process.

You retain intent, judgment, priorities, corrections, and acceptance of the outcome. AI can investigate, help plan, implement, test, verify, document, and preserve continuity—but it is not autonomous project authority.

Shared vocabulary
