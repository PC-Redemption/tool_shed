Focus

Campaigns

A campaign is the focused execution cycle that answers, “What are we accomplishing right now?” It keeps the outcome visible across planning, implementation, delivery, and review.

One outcome, explicit evidence

A durable campaign records a stable ID and number, lifecycle state, focus areas, dependencies, decisions, intended outcome, and an explicit completion gate. A campaign may implement part of a roadmap milestone or stand alone.

Campaigns provide execution. Plans, tests, builds, and deployments can be stages; none automatically replaces the campaign outcome.

Inner cycles return control upward

Tool Shed nests Program → Milestone Wave → Queue → Campaign → Evidence. Evidence returns to the campaign, campaign completion returns to the queue, an empty queue returns to its milestone/program/owner, and a passed milestone gate returns to the program. Only the intended outcome and every applicable gate complete the Program Cycle.

Origin is computed separately as direct, owner-originated, roadmap-derived, or detour. It does not choose Direct/Guided/Coordinated/Deep coordination or the work1–work5 endpoint.

Empty is not complete. Status, overview, and next share one Cycle State Capsule that names the owning cycle and safe next transition without approving or materializing anything.

Bare ts: next selects or resumes one campaign. If none is ready, it rolls control upward and reports Dangler Resolution, exact plan approval, milestone/gate review, next-milestone derivation, roadmap review/completion, or the owner choice that safely follows. It never performs those higher-level transitions implicitly.

Add queue positions for a bounded batch, use camp with zero-padded numbers or full IDs for stable references, or use * for the current active-queue snapshot. Resolved targets run sequentially; later additions are excluded, and every target keeps its own gate and authority boundary. Tool Shed stops at the first failure, blocker, decision, stale state, dependency, or missing authority and reports completed and remaining stable IDs.

Queue positions can move; the parenthesized zero-padded campaign number and full Campaign ID remain stable. Blocked work remains active, and lifecycle writes use fresh state tokens to reject stale mutations.

Dependencies and decisions

A campaign is ready only when its dependencies are complete and no owner decision blocks it. Deferral needs a reason and reactivation condition; abandonment preserves a disposition and replacement when applicable.

Continue through the authorized outcome

Campaign continuity means the agent keeps working while the next action is reversible, in scope, and already authorized. A useful checkpoint, passing test, successful build, or phase boundary is not automatically a review gate.

Roadmap relationships remain evidence relationships. A campaign can satisfy a gate or reveal that the roadmap should change; it does not silently rewrite approved direction.

Stop for a real boundary

Pause for a requested review, contradictory evidence, a material unresolved decision, new authority, or a protected, destructive, irreversible, or external action that was not authorized. Otherwise continue to the explicit completion gate.

A campaign completes only after that gate and applicable verification pass. Completion moves the request into history and refreshes active and completed queue projections as one recoverable lifecycle operation.
