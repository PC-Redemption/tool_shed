Workflow and cycles

The workflow is the operator path through Tool Shed. Five nested cycles govern how that path repeats, how completion is judged, and which level owns the next move.

Brainstorming can precede PRM

When an idea needs to evolve across sessions, the optional Brainstorm / Discovery Cycle preserves it in one living Idea Brief: capture, explore, compare, remember, and synthesize. ts: brainstorm and ts: bs are exact aliases.

Idea → Brainstorm / Discovery → PRM

Idea Briefs are indexed but remain outside campaign reconciliation. ts: prm idea <id-or-path> carries the synthesis forward without treating exploration as approval.

PRM means Plan → Roadmap → Milestone

PRM is Tool Shed's complete outer coordination lifecycle after an outcome is understood well enough to pursue. The Plan Cycle settles intent and project-map direction. The Roadmap Cycle owns the current Program Roadmap. Each Milestone Cycle derives, materializes, runs, and evaluates one evidence-gated milestone wave.

ts: prm <outcome> requests continuation through the full PRM until the intended outcome and applicable gates pass or genuine owner intervention is required. Roadmap and Milestone are the human-facing names for the compatible Program and Milestone Wave cycle states. Covered faithful transitions advance automatically under the active authority envelope; PRM does not grant release beyond the requested endpoint or bypass protected-target authority.

One workflow, five control cycles

The visible workflow moves from Project Map through roadmap direction, campaign planning, queue selection, execution, evidence, and review. The cycle model explains the control underneath that path:

Program → Milestone Wave → Queue → Campaign → Evidence

Each inner cycle serves its owner. Finishing an inner cycle returns control upward; it does not automatically complete the broader outcome.

Empty is not complete

An empty queue proves only that the Queue Cycle has no ready campaign. Control returns to the owning milestone, program, or owner to evaluate a gate, derive the next wave, review drift, resolve a pending plan, or decide what should happen next.

ts: overview, ts: status, and ts: next share one Cycle State Capsule that identifies the owning cycle and the safe next transition. Covered faithful transitions continue with fresh internal tokens; the capsule does not invent a material decision, expand the endpoint, or cross a protected boundary.

Completion belongs to the outer outcome. A successful command, campaign, queue, or milestone is evidence for its owner—not proof that the whole program is finished.

Cycles do not choose scope or authority

Cycle state answers which loop owns the next move. Keep it separate from three other questions:

  • Work origin: direct, owner-originated, roadmap-derived, or detour.
  • Coordination: Direct, Guided, Coordinated, or Deep.
  • Execution endpoint: work1 through work5.

A roadmap-derived campaign can still be Direct and stop at work1. Likewise, entering a higher cycle does not grant deployment, production, or approval authority.
