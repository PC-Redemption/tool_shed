Ideas and exploration

You can start before you know the design. Exploration makes the desired outcome clearer without pretending uncertainty is already resolved.

Incomplete intent is enough

An idea can be a problem, possibility, frustration, or desired change. Tool Shed treats it as a conversation entry point—not an implicit commitment to the first implementation mentioned.

Useful question: What should become true for the user when this is successful?

Explore before planning when

  • The outcome matters but the solution is still assumed.
  • Constraints, affected systems, or authority boundaries are unclear.
  • Several credible paths would produce meaningfully different results.

A clear, reversible, single-repository change may skip formal planning and proceed directly.

Choose ephemeral discussion or durable brainstorming

ts: discuss how a public documentation site should work

Discussion explores motivation, constraints, assumptions, unknowns, and the smallest useful next route without writing.

ts: brainstorm how a public documentation site should workts: bs idea public-documentation-site

brainstorm and bs are exact aliases. They create or resume one durable Idea Brief so the thinking survives across sessions. With schema-2 authority it is addressed by an immutable visible ID in SQLite; before cutover it remains a tracked Markdown file. A bare ts: brainstorm lists active briefs without mutation.

Common misuse

Do not treat brainstorming as approval to plan, build, deploy, or publish. Likewise, do not demand a complete specification before discovery can begin.

One living Idea Brief

The brief keeps a concise current synthesis above useful dated exploration notes. It can preserve possibilities, tradeoffs, constraints, non-goals, reminders, open questions, assumptions, and decisions without forcing every section to be complete.

Pre-PRM means outside the campaign queue. Promotion can open an owning outcome loop so later roadmap, campaign, release, and product evidence reconcile back to the original intent.

Review deliberately, not on every turn

Ordinary brainstorming stays responsive and never runs semantic readiness review. Use the explicit manual trigger when you want the brief evaluated.

ts: bs review IDEA-0008

The result is bound to the exact Idea revision and is reported as absent, stale, current-not-ready, or current-ready. A not-ready result focuses the dialogue on material blockers. Operational review failure stops promotion instead of pretending the idea is ready.

Exactly two triggers: the manual command above, and ts: prm idea when no current ready result exists.

Carry the result into PRM

When the outcome is coherent enough to pursue, use the brief as the durable source for Plan → Roadmap → Milestone. Visible uncertainty remains visible; promotion does not independently expand the authority envelope.

ts: prm idea public-documentation-site

PRM reuses a current ready result or starts the focused review. Any named readiness gates must transfer exactly into accepted map or roadmap state. The brief remains ready-for-PRM until settled project-map direction captures it, then becomes promoted provenance linked to that map. Covered faithful transitions continue automatically.
