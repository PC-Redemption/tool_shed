Select the first ready campaign

ts: next
What it reads
A freshly validated owner queue plus current Program Roadmap state.
What it changes
Starts the selected campaign and executes only that campaign. With no ready campaign, it changes nothing.
Approval required
The request authorizes the selected campaign at its natural endpoint; higher-level approvals remain separate.
Success looks like
One ready campaign is working, or the Capsule identifies the exact safe plan, milestone, program, or owner transition without guessing.
