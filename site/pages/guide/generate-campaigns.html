Preview exactly. Continue when covered.

Campaign-plan preview cannot change the queue. A faithful covered plan uses its fresh token internally, materializes, and continues only through the requested endpoint.

Derive a milestone plan

Replace M1 with the milestone ID from the approved roadmap.

ts: derive campaigns for milestone M1
Required state
An approved roadmap containing the milestone.
What it reads
The approved milestone, gates, dependencies, and queue state.
What it changes
Nothing; it previews a campaign manifest.
Approval required
No.
Success looks like
An exact dependency-aware plan and manifest token.

Materialize the exact campaign plan

Level 3 applies a faithful in-envelope plan automatically with its internal manifest token. The explicit token command remains available for manual level-0 operation.

ts: approve campaign plan <token>
What it reads
The unchanged manifest, current roadmap/queue state, and authority envelope.
What it changes
Creates the planned campaigns in the queue and may continue into execution when the endpoint covers it.
Approval required
Only for a material decision, new authority, or insufficient autonomy.
Success looks like
Campaigns materialize and covered execution continues without a ceremonial pause.
